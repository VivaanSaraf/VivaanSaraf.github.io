What is Open Duck Mini V2?

Open Duck Mini V2 is a small biped robot: “biped” means it walks on two legs. To walk, its leg motors have to move together while the robot tracks its body position. An IMU is a motion sensor that helps measure which way the body is tilted. A Raspberry Pi is the small computer that runs the control software.

In progress

Preparing for a walking test

I am working on an Open Duck Mini V2, but getting a two-legged robot to walk starts well before the first step. Its Raspberry Pi needs to communicate with the motors so the software can tell each leg how to move. I am working through that wiring and communication setup first.

I am also checking the IMU, which measures the robot's tilt, and working through the servo offsets that set safe starting positions for its joints. After that, I need to connect the Xbox controller and check how the robot responds to commands.

My next milestone is a carefully calibrated walking test. I have not reached that test yet; right now I am making sure the connections and starting positions are ready for it.

Current stage: I am preparing the motors, motion sensor, and controller. A walking test is still ahead.
