What is Open Duck Mini V2?

Open Duck Mini V2 is a small biped robot: “biped” means it walks on two legs. To walk, its leg motors have to move together while the robot tracks its body position. An IMU is a motion sensor that helps measure which way the body is tilted. A Raspberry Pi is the small computer that runs the control software.
