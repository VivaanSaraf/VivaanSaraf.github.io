What is a drone?

A drone is an aircraft without a pilot on board. Its frame holds the parts together, a propulsion system pushes it through the air, a power source supplies energy, and controls adjust how it moves. The exact parts vary between builds; I will add the parts used in my drone when I have those details ready.

Building a drone

I built a drone as a hands-on project.
