About Me

My Coding Journey

My interest in technology grew through hands-on robotics and programming at MakerWorks. I worked on VEX IQ in 2024 and 2025, learning how mechanical design and code come together on a competition field. In my second VEX IQ year, my team placed 2nd at Nationals.

I have also built a drone and a two-wheel autonomous Arduino robot with an ultrasonic sensor and servo. More recently, I have been setting up an SO-101 robot arm with LeRobot and working through the motors, sensors, and Raspberry Pi setup for an Open Duck Mini V2. I built this portfolio with HTML and CSS to share what I learn.
