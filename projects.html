My Projects

Competition robots, robot arms, a walking robot, a drone, and an Arduino-based autonomous robot. Each project has taught me something new about turning an idea into hardware that works.

Project 01

VEX IQ Robotics

VEX IQ is a robotics platform for building robots that solve challenges on a field. Our team built one that could drive, collect game pieces, and use a fast-spinning wheel called a flywheel.

I used VEXcode Blocks to control the flywheel with controller and sensor inputs, then tested the robot with my team.

Result: My team placed 2nd at Nationals in my second VEX IQ year.

VEX IQ · VEXcode Blocks · Motors · Sensors
